## 4. Grade recommendation calibration - supported vs unsupported

- The teacher's four approved *synthetic pattern anchors* remain **R -> Y -> G = C+**, **G -> G -> G = A-**, **Y -> G -> G = B+**, **R -> no judgment -> Y = D+**, under comparable meaningful/independent, sufficient scope and without a chapter test. They are not lookup rules based on counts.
- Strong work on two short binomial rectangles is eligible for favorable **task judgments**, but **does not certify a high LG 3.2 letter grade** without coverage of what has actually been taught and the degree of independence. Do not penalize or alter the existing grade automatically; flag coverage uncertainty.
- A correct later representative checkpoint can support major upward movement without replaying a skill two or three more times. Conversely, a single apparent weak outcome after earlier strong independent work is a **contradiction review**, not an automatic drop.
- Before convincing chapter-test evidence, no automatic A; A-minus is the maximum provisional recommendation. D/D-/F remain uncalibrated and are never created from blank or uncertain work.
- The intended four Quick Checks per week are **evidence opportunities**, not four weekly grades or four required green votes per student. Their front/back can contribute to different LG files.
