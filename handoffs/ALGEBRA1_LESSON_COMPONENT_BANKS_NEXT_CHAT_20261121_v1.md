1. **Assessment/skill evidence bank** — teacher-approved direct-skill questions and extensions, summative multiple-choice; original CPM Review & Preview remains a reference source, not automatically used as warm-ups.
2. **Warm-Up bank** — source-lesson Q3/Q4 spiral families and linked optional Q5/Q6 extensions; Q1/Q2 fluency choice bank remains as established. ## Proposed new banks, to discuss before implementation
3. **Learning Focus / Notes** — approved lesson I Can, directly relevant vocabulary, Methods & Meanings; support today's lesson only. Use the approved layout in `maps_memories`. Full editability and teacher AI modification request.
4. **EX / YTI** — paired worked example and closely matched try-it on the **current** lesson I Can; number swaps and alternative examples; MathJax, approved CSS, step-by-step solution control.
5. **Launch** — 2–3 teacher-selectable CPM Launch/Explore-first choices, normally 15–30 minutes for actual investigation and discovery; teacher edit and AI request. Respect CPM provenance/reference, don't transcribe without checking.
6. **Smarty Pants (optional)** — substantive challenge tied to **today's** lesson, not Q5/Q6 spiral. Enrichment modes from Exeter/IMP/POD/Math Labs and curated resources: worthwhile application, design, compare decisions, conjecture, test a model.
7. **Twelve-question sets and other practices** — one mastered set can drive distinct activity formats (projection, workstations, find-someone-who, etc.); authoring and answer key separate from format; preserve two questions per printed Projection Set page where selected.
8. **Reflections / Exit Ticket** — short 3-minute prompts, 1–4 readiness scale and genuine open reflection; printable two-per-page layout, minimal decoration, no forced extra math problem.
