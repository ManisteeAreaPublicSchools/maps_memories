## Decisions and method

- Retained **29 broad teacher-tracked I-can goals** in **25 sections** and the approved **107 core periods**.
- Curated **144 distinct observable supporting skills** (previously 132) without requiring additional tracked grades.
- Grounded the curriculum in the nine publisher `LearningOutcomes.docx` source files (Passes 1–2), selected original Conceptual Physics Course Pack Think Sheets and lab directions, public Physics Classroom Tutorial and Lesson Plans references, plus sampled Hewitt chapter/section extracts.
- Direct source text inspection covered over forty selected purchased Think Sheets and multiple labs across all nine retained units. These are **samples spanning every approved section**, not a claim that every purchased figure, optional worksheet, or teacher guide was visually verified. One oversized Position-Time Graphs Lab DOCX did not extract cleanly; the purchased slope Think Sheet and other section sources supplied the cross-check. The first pages of three representative purchased PDF worksheets were also visually checked (Kinematics8 velocity-time graphs, Energy6 work-energy bar charts, and Optics7 concave-mirror ray diagrams). This does not constitute visual verification of every publisher diagram.
- No purchased source pages, answer keys, scanned student work, or private roster data are placed in the ZIP.
- Preserve the broad I-can as the progress address; use supporting-skill `domain` plus `question_bank_guidance` metadata to design nonredundant evidence.

### Guardrails for Question Bank planning

1. Start with a **goal**, select a precise supporting skill, and specify what observable evidence would demonstrate it.
2. Use distinct families (physical explanation, graph/data, diagram/model, experiment/CER, contextual one-step calculation) only where suited. No quotas or superficial wording variants.
3. Do not turn an identification skill into a second item by merely appending “justify.” Instead require a different physical inference or representation.
4. Preserve signed direction, units, system boundaries and conceptual errors where physically relevant.
5. Lab completion itself is not mastery evidence; analyze a prediction, table, graph, discrepancy or CER.
6. The approved teaching days, publisher course cuts, and lesson availability constrain all question scope.
7. Secure assessments require independently authored problems; **no protected publisher text should be copied to public files**.
8. Teacher approval and item-by-item Question Bank source QA remain a later pass.

**PHY-LG-1.1 — I can investigate relationships between variables and support scientific conclusions with organized data, evidence, and reasoning.**
