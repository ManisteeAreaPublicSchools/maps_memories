## What was verified

## Rules for using the mapping

1. Track progress only against the 29 broad I-cans; individual supporting skills are diagnostic targets, not 144 separate grades.
2. Identify the exact evidence claim before choosing a problem. One source candidate is not guaranteed to assess every supporting skill.
3. Prefer meaningful contrasts in cognitive demand and representation, not repetitive question shells with added “explain/justify.”
4. Lab completion alone does not demonstrate understanding. Capture a prediction, diagram, graph, discrepancy analysis, or CER grounded in collected evidence.
5. Choose realistic paper-first formats with drawing/writing room. Print duplex, scan and review with the teacher; no automatic scoring or student distribution is activated.
6. Individual purchased questions need content, mathematics, diagram, answer-key, accessibility, and licensing checks before reuse; licensed text and images stay in private source folders.
7. Preserve sections and time allocations; evidence choices can be consolidated into instructional labs or review periods. No dates or extra testing days have been added.

## Coverage gaps and source limitations

- **Chapter 1:** No dedicated purchased Conceptual Course bank for identifying variables, CER, graph families, or graph-prediction investigations. Use original questions grounded in Dune Buggy/Stopping Distance/pendulum evidence.
- **2.3:** Publisher graph questions are useful conceptual candidates; an original graph task must test signed area and translation between x-t, v-t, dot diagrams and words.
- **3.3:** Newtons Laws bank candidates do not replace coffee-filter/terminal-speed evidence; never credit a coffee-filter task for third-law force pairs.
- **4.3:** A candidate projectile vector item does not prove a student can make an empirical launch prediction; use the Hit the Target design and a backup measurement data set.
- **5.2/5.3:** Avoid mass ratio/total momentum item clones; distinguish explosion interactions, hit-and-stick conservation, and bouncing direction changes.
- **6.1/6.3:** Work-direction, energy quantities, conservation, and bar-chart accounting are different ideas; do not collapse them into only KE/PE arithmetic.
- **10.2/10.3-B:** Use original fixed/free-end wave drawings and resonance-lab evidence; full string/air-column harmonic sets are outside core.
- **12.2:** No confirmed curved-mirror bank category; prioritize original concave/convex ray diagrams and Finding Smiley/Optics Bench evidence. Refraction/lenses must not leak into the test.
- **9.3:** Purchased source has useful comparisons, but original branch-current diagrams are needed; advanced parallel equivalent-resistance network math is optional/out of core.
